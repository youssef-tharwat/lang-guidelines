# deprecation

Mark deprecated APIs with `@deprecated` plus clear directions for fixing call sites.

**Why:** Callers learn how to migrate where they look.

**Sources:** G:deprecation

---

## Deprecation (G:deprecation)

[Google TypeScript Style Guide](https://google.github.io/styleguide/tsguide.html#deprecation), CC BY 3.0.

Mark deprecated methods, classes or interfaces with an `@deprecated` JSDoc annotation. A deprecation comment must include simple, clear directions for people to fix their call sites.
